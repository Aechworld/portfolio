## Research

**Job:** "How do they investigate before designing?"

Describe / answer:

- **Reference works** — what specific things did you look at? (games, films, books, projects,
artists). For each: name it + one line on what you took from it (a mechanic, a tone, a
structure, a thing to avoid). Be honest about depth (studied vs. glanced).
- **Conceptual/theoretical input** — any texts, theories, facts, historical material? Name
them. (If a collaborator led this, say so and get their list.)
- **Visual/material reference** — what fed the look/feel? Specific archives, photographers,
collections? What was your method (e.g. "referenced real images as ground truth, built custom
where they fell short")?
- **Honest note:** If research was thin, say what you actually did rather than inflating it.

## Synthesis

**Job:** "After all that input, what did they DECIDE the thing would be?"

Describe / answer:

- **Core thesis / stance** — one statement of what this project believes or is built around.
- **Design principles / pillars** — the 2–4 rules that drove every decision. For each, write
the principle as a short claim, then 1–3 sentences unpacking it. A real principle rules things
*out* — note what each one forbids, not just what it values.
- **Self-imposed constraints** — anything you deliberately refused to do? (no narration, no UI,
no scale, etc.)

## Iteration

**Job:** "Do they change their mind when the work pushes back?" (Reviewers dwell here.)

Describe / answer:

- List the versions/major changes in order. For EACH version:
    - **Name + one-line description** of what it was
    - **What it tried to do** (the intent)
    - **What worked / what didn't** (honest)
    - **Why it changed** — and *what kind* of reason: concept wrong? scope too big? feedback?
    A *concept* cut and a *scope* cut are different stories — name which.
    - **What changed for the next version**
- Opening line: was this a smooth refinement, or real pivots? Set that expectation up front.
- Artifacts: screenshots/builds/footage of old versions. List what exists.
