A dummy app which brings gig-worker data to the limelight for customers.

April 2026 · 2 weeks  ·  Nikhil, Janvi, Yadhu  ·  Class brief  ·  Figma, Claude

Context

The app provokes the user while giving them a sense of scale and impact that gig workers are facing. It is designed to serve as advocacy — pressuring managers and business owners to act on the conditions their workers operate in.

Final Outcome

A Zepto clone that provokes people to think about the situation of gig workers delivering orders in high-AQI conditions. The declining health of these workers affects them in the long term. Companies provide no safety measures for this situation and no health insurance.
